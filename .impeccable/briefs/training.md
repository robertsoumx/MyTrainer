# Training and range
Mode: Operate for the library; Experience during the drill.
## Direction contract
THESIS: Four directly playable aim exercises replace the sixteen-skill catalog, tactical quizzes and branching rounds.
OWN-WORLD: Keep the graphite/coral app shell, simplify it to four navigation destinations. Inside the range, matte blue architecture, smooth luminous spheres and narrow cyan light strips. No realistic materials, logos, humanoids or gun models.
STORY: Choose a drill, read one instruction, enter full viewport, aim and finish a 30-second run. Result gives score, accuracy and a single retry/next action.
FIRST VIEWPORT: Training title, profile context, four large drill previews with one sentence each and a start action. Progress only where real data exists. In game: full-viewport room, center crosshair, time/score/accuracy in a compact top HUD; pause and exit remain obvious.
FORM: User-pinned Aim Lab convention; code implementation extends the existing app. Launcher unavailable; no seed is fabricated. Signature interaction: immediate target removal plus a short spatial impact ring, with no camera animation.
FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
